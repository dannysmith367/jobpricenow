Frequently Asked Questions

Frequently Asked Questions
Does it account for the number of coats?

Yes — describe how many coats the job needs (like "two coats" or "primer plus two coats") and it factors into the labor and material estimate.

What about prep work or drywall repair before painting?

Mention it in the job description — patching, sanding, or drywall repair before paint all adds labor time, and the estimate accounts for that.

Is this just for interior, or exterior too?

Both. Describe an interior or exterior paint job and it'll price accordingly — see the interior and exterior guides for typical ranges.

Do I need to sign up?

No. No account, no login, no email required to get a price.
