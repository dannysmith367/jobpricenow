Frequently Asked Questions
How much should a handyman charge per hour?

It depends heavily on location, experience, and the type of work, but hourly rates typically reflect labor cost, overhead, and local market conditions. JobPriceNow factors these in when it estimates a job rather than relying on one flat hourly number.

How much should I charge for a small handyman job?

Small jobs often carry a minimum service charge to account for travel time and overhead, even when the actual labor is brief. Describe the job above to get a realistic estimate that accounts for this.

Is it better to charge hourly or by the job?

It depends on the work. Open-ended or unpredictable repairs are often priced hourly, while clearly defined installations are frequently better suited to a flat rate. Read more in our flat rate vs. hourly rate guide.

How do I estimate a handyman job?

Describe the job, enter your ZIP code, and add photos if they'd help provide context. JobPriceNow weighs labor, materials, and difficulty to return a realistic price range in seconds.

Can JobPriceNow estimate local handyman pricing?

Yes. Entering a ZIP code helps JobPriceNow account for regional pricing differences rather than returning a flat, nationwide estimate.

Can I use photos when getting a job estimate?

Yes, you can add up to three photos. This is optional, but it can help provide extra context for jobs that are harder to describe in text alone.

Can I create a professional quote for my customer?

Yes. Once you have an estimate, JobPriceNow offers the option to create a professional, branded quote you can send to a customer for $2.99.
