About JobPriceNow

Built by a contractor. Not a tech company.

My name is Dan Smith. Before I built any of this, I spent years doing handyman and contractor work — the actual jobs, not just the business side of it.

If you've done this kind of work, you already know the part that never gets easier: standing in front of a job and figuring out what to charge for it. It's not the painting, the drywall, or the install that trips people up. It's the number. Price too low and you're working for free by the time you factor in materials and how long it actually took. Price too high off a bad guess and you lose the job to someone who priced it right.

I ran into that problem constantly. Not because I didn't know my trade — because pricing a job well takes weighing a dozen things at once: labor time, material cost, how difficult the job actually is versus how it looks on paper, what's normal to charge in your area, and what a customer will actually accept.

So I built the tool I wished I'd had

JobPriceNow is a free calculator that takes a real job description, your ZIP code, and — if it helps — a few photos, and turns that into a realistic price range. It's built on the same kind of thinking I used to do in my head standing in someone's driveway, just faster and more consistent.

It's not trying to replace your judgment. You still know your customers, your overhead, and the specifics of the job better than any calculator ever will. It's meant to give you a solid starting point instead of a guess.

Why it's free

No signup, no account, no catch. Describe the job and get a price. That's the whole idea — I wanted something a contractor could actually use on-site in under a minute, not another app to manage.

Still building this in the open

JobPriceNow is genuinely a work in progress, and I'd rather build it with input from people who actually do this work than guess in a vacuum. If you try it on a job you've already priced and the number is way off — or surprisingly close — I want to hear about it. See the contact page for how to reach me.

— Dan Smith
Founder, JobPriceNow
