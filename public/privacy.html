Privacy Policy

Last updated: 2026

JobPriceNow ("we," "us") provides a free job-pricing estimator for handymen. This page explains what we collect and how we use it.

What we collect

When you use the estimator, we process the job description, ZIP code, and any photos you choose to upload in order to generate a price estimate. Photos and descriptions may be sent to our AI provider for analysis and are not permanently stored by default in this version of the product.

Photos

Uploaded photos may contain images of your customer's property. We compress and transmit only what's needed to generate an estimate, and we do not publish photos publicly.

Analytics

We collect basic, privacy-conscious usage analytics (such as which steps of the estimator are used) to improve the product. We do not sell personal data.

Contact

Questions about this policy can be directed to the site owner via JobPriceNow.com.
